Frequently asked questions

  • Where are my tasks stored? Only on your device. Visdap has no account and no cloud server, so we cannot see or restore your tasks.
  • How do I restore Visdap Pro on a new iPhone? Open Visdap → Pro → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.
  • How do I cancel or manage my subscription? On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Visdap Pro. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Reminders don't sound. Allow notifications for Visdap in Settings → Notifications. For loud reminders on iOS 26, also allow Alarms for Visdap.
  • Calendar events are not shown. Allow calendar access for Visdap in Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars. Visdap only reads events; it never changes them.
