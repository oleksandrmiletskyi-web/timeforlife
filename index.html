Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Time for Life apps handle your information. It covers three packages published on Google Play:

  • Time for Life for Android phones and tablets (com.timeforlife.app)
  • Time for Life for Wear OS watches (the watch version of the same app)
  • Time for Life 24h watch face for Wear OS (com.timeforlife.app.watchface)

The apps are published by Woder ("we", "us").

1. The short version

  • We do not have servers. The apps do not send your data to us.
  • There is no account and no sign-up.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking tools in the apps.
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone and, if you use one, on your own watch.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

2. Information stored on your device

The phone app saves the following in its private storage on your device:

  • tasks and plans you create (titles, descriptions, dates, times, reminders, colors, priorities);
  • day sectors and deadline projects (names, dates, colors, planned hours);
  • your date of birth and expected lifespan, if you enter them for the optional life counter;
  • app settings (language, theme, clock appearance, sound and vibration choices, widget and floating clock position);
  • a copy of an audio file, if you choose your own reminder sound;
  • your Pro status (whether a Google Play subscription is active and which product it is).

This information is not transmitted to us or to any third party.

3. Permissions and how they are used

Calendar (read only). If you turn on calendar sync in the planner, the app reads events from the calendars on your device (title, description, start and end time, color) to show them on your 24-hour dial and in your day plan. Events are read on your device, kept in memory while the app is running, and are not saved to the app's files or sent anywhere. The app cannot create, change or delete calendar events. You can turn sync off in the app or revoke the permission in system settings at any time.

Usage access (optional). If you allow usage access, the app checks which app is currently in the foreground for one purpose only: to hide the floating clock while you are on your home screen where the Time for Life widget is already placed. Before the system screen opens, the app shows an explanation and asks for your consent. Only the name of the foreground app is read; it is kept in memory, is not saved and is not sent anywhere. Without this access everything else works as usual. You can revoke it at any time in system settings.

Display over other apps (optional). Used to show the floating 24-hour clock above other apps after you turn it on. The clock does not read the content of other apps or your touches in them.

Foreground service. While the floating clock or the home-screen clock widget is in use, a service with a permanent notification keeps the clock hands moving. It does not collect data.

Notifications, alarms, full-screen alerts, vibration. Used to remind you about your tasks at the time you chose.

Start after reboot. Used to restore your reminders and the floating clock after the phone restarts.

List of home-screen apps. The app asks the system which installed apps are home screens (launchers). This is used only for the auto-hide feature described above.

Internet and network state. Used by Google Play Billing to show prices and process subscriptions. The app itself makes no other network requests.

Watch app permissions. The watch app requests vibration, wake lock and start-after-reboot permissions. It has no internet permission.

Watch face. The Time for Life 24h watch face contains no code, requests no permissions and collects nothing.

4. Synchronization with your watch

If you use a Wear OS watch with Time for Life, the phone app sends data to your own watch through the Wear OS Data Layer, a connection between your paired devices provided by Google Play services. The data sent is: your language and clock appearance settings, your date of birth and expected lifespan (for the life counter), your deadline projects (names and dates), and today's day sectors and tasks (titles, times, colors, priorities, completion state). The watch stores this data locally and can send back task completion and watch settings changes. We do not receive any of this data.

5. Purchases

Time for Life Pro is a subscription sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is processed by Google; we do not receive or store your card details or your Google account information. The app stores only the fact that a subscription is active and the product identifier, on your device. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data. We do not use advertising or analytics providers.

7. Backups

If device backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include this app's data in the backup of your device that is stored in your Google account. This backup is controlled by you and by Google, not by us.

8. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by:

  • deleting individual tasks, projects or settings in the app;
  • clearing the app's storage in Android settings (Settings > Apps > Time for Life > Storage > Clear storage);
  • uninstalling the app from your phone and from your watch.

Because we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

9. Children

Time for Life is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
