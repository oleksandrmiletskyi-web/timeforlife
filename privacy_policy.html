Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the app Visdap: Visual Day Planner (formerly named Time for Life) and explains how it handles your information. It covers:

  • Visdap: Visual Day Planner for Android phones and tablets — Google Play package com.timeforlife.app;
  • Visdap for Wear OS watches — the watch version of the same app, published under the same package;
  • the 24h watch face that the watch app can install on your watch, and the separate watch face package com.timeforlife.app.watchface;
  • Visdap for iPhone and for Mac, distributed through the Apple App Store and Mac App Store (app identifier com.timeforlife.app), including their widgets — see the section “Visdap for iPhone and Mac” below.

Who we are

The app is developed and published on Google Play by Woder — the developer name of Oleksandr Miletskyi, an individual developer ("we", "us"). Woder is the data controller for the purposes of this policy. Contact details are in section 12.

1. The short version

  • We do not have servers. The apps do not send your data to us.
  • There is no account and no sign-up.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking tools in the apps.
  • Everything you enter stays on your device (phone, watch, iPhone or Mac).
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or by Apple. We never see your payment details.

2. Information stored on your device

The phone app saves the following in its private storage on your device:

  • tasks and plans you create (titles, descriptions, dates, times, reminders, colors, priorities);
  • day sectors and deadline projects (names, dates, colors, planned hours);
  • your date of birth and expected lifespan, if you enter them for the optional life counter;
  • app settings (language, theme, clock appearance, sound and vibration choices, widget and floating clock position);
  • a copy of an audio file, if you choose your own reminder sound;
  • your Pro status (whether a Google Play subscription is active and which product it is).

This information is not transmitted to us or to any third party.

3. Permissions and how they are used

Calendar (read only). If you turn on calendar sync in the planner, the app reads events from the calendars on your device (title, description, start and end time, color) to show them on your 24-hour dial and in your day plan. Events are read on your device, kept in memory while the app is running, and are not saved to the app's files or sent anywhere. The app cannot create, change or delete calendar events. You can turn sync off in the app or revoke the permission in system settings at any time.

Usage access (optional). If you allow usage access, the app checks which app is currently in the foreground for one purpose only: to hide the floating clock while you are on your home screen where the Visdap widget is already placed. Before the system screen opens, the app shows an explanation and asks for your consent. Only the name of the foreground app is read; it is kept in memory, is not saved and is not sent anywhere. Without this access everything else works as usual. You can revoke it at any time in system settings.

Display over other apps (optional). Used to show the floating 24-hour clock above other apps after you turn it on. The clock does not read the content of other apps or your touches in them.

Foreground service. While the floating clock or the home-screen clock widget is in use, a service with a permanent notification keeps the clock hands moving. It does not collect data.

Notifications, alarms, full-screen alerts, vibration. Used to remind you about your tasks at the time you chose.

Start after reboot. Used to restore your reminders and the floating clock after the phone restarts.

List of home-screen apps. The app asks the system which installed apps are home screens (launchers). This is used only for the auto-hide feature described above.

Internet and network state. Used by Google Play Billing to show prices and process subscriptions. The app itself makes no other network requests.

Watch app permissions. The watch app requests vibration, wake lock and start-after-reboot permissions, and the Wear OS permissions to install its own 24h watch face on your watch and set it as active when you ask for it (Watch Face Push). It has no internet permission.

Watch face. The 24h watch face contains no code, requests no permissions and collects nothing.

4. Synchronization with your watch

If you use a Wear OS watch with Visdap, the phone app sends data to your own watch through the Wear OS Data Layer, a connection between your paired devices provided by Google Play services. The data sent is: your language and clock appearance settings, your date of birth and expected lifespan (for the life counter), your deadline projects (names and dates), and today's day sectors and tasks (titles, times, colors, priorities, completion state). The watch stores this data locally and can send back task completion and watch settings changes. We do not receive any of this data.

5. Purchases

Visdap Pro is a subscription sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is processed by Google; we do not receive or store your card details or your Google account information. The app stores only the fact that a subscription is active and the product identifier, on your device. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Visdap for iPhone and Mac

Storage. On iPhone and Mac the app keeps your data (tasks, projects, settings, Pro status) in a file inside the app’s own sandboxed container, shared only with the app’s own widgets through an App Group on the same device. The data is not synchronized between your iPhone and your Mac and is not sent to us or to iCloud by the app.

Calendar (optional, read only). If you turn on “Calendar events”, the app asks for access to your calendars and reads event titles, times and colors on your device to show them in the planner and on the clock face. Events are not saved to the app’s files, not changed and not sent anywhere. You can turn this off in the app or in Settings / System Settings › Privacy at any time.

Notifications and alarms. Used to remind you about your tasks at the time you chose. On iPhone with iOS 26 or later, tasks marked as loud reminders can use the system alarm (AlarmKit) after you allow it.

Mac only. The optional launch-at-login setting, the floating clock and the global shortcut for quick task entry work locally and read no content of other apps. Backup export and import use only the files you choose in the system file dialog.

Purchases. Visdap Pro on iPhone and Mac is an auto-renewable subscription sold by Apple through your Apple Account. Payment is processed by Apple; we do not receive your payment details or your Apple Account information. The app stores only whether a subscription is active and its product identifier. Apple’s handling of your data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

No tracking. The iPhone and Mac apps contain no advertising, analytics or tracking code and do not collect data as defined by Apple’s App Privacy requirements.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone. We do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting providers. The only third parties involved are Google, as the provider of Google Play Billing (purchases), Wear OS Data Layer (phone-to-watch sync between your own devices) and Android backup, under the Google Privacy Policy, and Apple, as the provider of App Store purchases and device backups on iPhone and Mac, under the Apple Privacy Policy.

7. Security

Your data is stored only in the app's private storage on your device, which Android, iOS and macOS isolate from other apps. The app has no servers and does not transmit your data over the internet, so there is no server-side copy that could be exposed. Data synchronized with your own watch travels over the encrypted Wear OS Data Layer connection between your paired devices. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play over Google's secure connection.

8. Backups

If device backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include this app's data in the backup of your device that is stored in your Google account. This backup is controlled by you and by Google, not by us.

9. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by:

  • deleting individual tasks, projects or settings in the app;
  • clearing the app's storage in Android settings (Settings > Apps > Visdap > Storage > Clear storage);
  • uninstalling the app from your phone and from your watch.

Because we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

10. Children

Visdap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
